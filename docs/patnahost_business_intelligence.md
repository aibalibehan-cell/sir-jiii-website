## 3. Commercial Model & Pricing Strategy

Unlike commoditized software-as-a-service (SaaS) providers with rigid pricing tables, PatnaHost operates on an **inquiry-driven bespoke consultation model**:
1. **Chat Over Chai / Free Consultation**: Prospective clients are encouraged to discuss business goals via phone or WhatsApp.
2. **Free Demo & Framing**: Prospective layouts and prototypes are shown before full commitment.
3. **Milestone Delivery**:
   - Step 1: Planning & Architecture Definition
   - Step 2: Design & Framing with Revisions
   - Step 3: Functional Integration & Error Testing
   - Step 4: Server Upload, Deployment & Ongoing Support Maintenance
4. **Transparent Project Quotes**: Pricing is customized according to complexity (e.g., static landing page vs database-backed e-commerce vs educational LMS).
